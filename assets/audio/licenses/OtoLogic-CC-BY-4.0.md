## Required attribution

When an OtoLogic material is used in a published work, retain this credit in
the video, description, application screen, README, or another place that is
reasonably linked to the work:

> 音素材：OtoLogic (https://otologic.jp/) / CC BY 4.0

The application also writes this text to
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES_AUDIO.txt` whenever an OtoLogic item is selected.

## Distribution and modification

OtoLogic's official FAQ states that commercial use and modification are
permitted and that the source material may be redistributed when the required
credit is preserved. No additional restriction may be imposed that prevents a
recipient from exercising the CC BY 4.0 rights. Do not register a work using
these shared materials with Content ID or otherwise claim exclusive rights in
the source material.

The starter pack extracts the selected MP3 files byte-for-byte from OtoLogic's
official ZIP downloads. Gain, looping, cue placement, and encoding can be
applied only while generating the user's video/audio outputs; those output
changes are recorded in the generated provenance files.
