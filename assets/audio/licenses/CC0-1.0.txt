CC0 1.0 Universal
=================

The audio files in the cc0-starter pack are identified by their source pages
as released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain
dedication.

Official summary:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Official legal code:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

CC0 does not require attribution. Source and creator metadata is retained in
the installed manifest for provenance and voluntary credit.
