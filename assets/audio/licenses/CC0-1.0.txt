CC0 1.0 Universal
=================

The CC0 audio files in the short-video-starter pack are identified by their
source pages as released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
public-domain dedication. Other files in this pack can use a different
license; see SOURCES.md and the installed manifest for each asset.

Official summary:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Official legal code:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

CC0 does not require attribution. Source and creator metadata is retained in
the installed manifest for provenance and voluntary credit.
